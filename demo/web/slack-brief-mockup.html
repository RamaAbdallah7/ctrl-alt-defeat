DIR'A‑PM

Decide · Defend · Deliver

A project-management copilot that lives in the team’s Slack channel. It runs the PM math that would otherwise be scattered across spreadsheets and MS Project — critical path, weighted decision matrix, earned value — and hands back one consolidated brief. The calculations run in deterministic Python; the AI only routes and consolidates; a human always makes the call.
